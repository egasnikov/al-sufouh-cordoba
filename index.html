Stages and gates

Dubai rules that shape the cash flow: the land must be fully paid and registered to the developer before the project is registered with RERA; buyers' money goes into the project escrow account and is released only against certified progress; before selling off-plan the developer posts a deposit against construction cost (modelled at 30% in cash, adjustable); surplus in escrow is released after completion; 5% of collections stays in escrow for 12 months after handover. Existing tenants must receive 12 months' notarised notice for a demolition eviction (Law No. 26 of 2007, Article 25).

Returns and LP / GP split

House waterfall on equity: 8% a year preferred return to the LP, the band from 8 to 10% to the GP, then 80 / 20 to a 25% IRR and 70 / 30 above; hurdles accrue monthly on actual contributions and distributions. GP fees (1% acquisition advisory on land and DLD, 1% disposition on GDV) are project costs in the GP economics. Corporate tax at 9% is shown for reference; the headline figures are pre-tax, as in our other models.

Before an offer

Open items

  1. Price evidence. A broker price opinion for new-build estates of 12,000 to 32,000 sq ft in Al Sufouh 2, and the DLD register for Emirates Hills, Jumeirah Islands, Al Barari and District One new builds over the last 24 months. Every other question is second to this one: the base price sits well above the inland evidence, and the price and land hurdles below show how much room there is.
  2. Ownership and land price. Who owns Cordoba Residence (single owner or strata), whether a bulk sale is possible, and what the AED 1,080 per sq ft of plot rests on: so far it is an estimate. The land price for a 20% margin is the negotiating anchor.
  3. Affection plan and DDA rules. Confirm GFA 89,899 sq m, G+3, plot subdivision into 46 estate plots, basements, setbacks and whether the clubhouse sits inside the GFA.
  4. Vacant possession. Tenancy schedule, lease expiries and the rent roll; the 12-month notice starts only after the transfer, so it sets the earliest demolition date.
  5. Product. Test the mix with an architect and two ultra-prime brokers: twelve homes of 32,000 sq ft at well over AED 100M each is a thin buyer pool. A mix weighted to 12,000 to 18,000 sq ft homes may sell faster at a higher rate per sq ft.
  6. Cost plan. Quantity surveyor estimate for shell and core, fit-out, basements, infrastructure and the clubhouse, with phasing into two or three releases.
  7. Sales strategy and absorption. The model sells homes a month; confirm with the brokers what the top of the market absorbs, and whether phased releases or selling the clubhouse phase first help.
  8. Financing. Land and construction debt terms from a private credit lender and a UAE bank for an escrow-compatible construction facility; debt only adds value while the project IRR is well above its cost.
  9. Developer status and tax. Developer licence, RERA registration, escrow bank and trustee, deposit in cash or bank guarantee, and the SPV's corporate tax position.

Method. Monthly model, month 0 is the land transfer. Land: price per sq ft of plot times 929,247 sq ft, plus 4% DLD and land brokerage (base 0%), plus the GP acquisition advisory fee of 1% of land and DLD. Existing rent, if entered, is collected until vacant possession. Demolition (15 per sq ft of plot) is paid evenly from vacant possession to construction start. Construction budget: residential GFA at one rate including fit-out and furniture (base 1,500 per sq ft), marketed area above GFA at 50% of that rate, clubhouse 43,056 sq ft at 900, infrastructure 45 and landscaping 35 per sq ft of plot, contingency on buildings, or on all works with the additional cost lines switched on; paid on a sine S-curve with a 10% advance at start and 10% retention, half released at handover and half 12 months after completion. Design 6% and project management 5% of buildings and clubhouse; developer overhead per year until handover. Additional cost lines (switch, off in the base case): authority fees and connections 3% (40% at permit, 60% at 60% progress), RERA, escrow and legal AED 10M before launch, defects reserve 1% six months after handover, 20 per sq ft a year on unsold marketed area after handover. Sales: a share in the launch month, then evenly to the end of the sales period, all tiers pro rata; broker commission and the 1% GP disposition fee at sale; marketing 35% around launch and the rest evenly. Buyer payments: deposit at sale, construction share evenly to the month before handover, handover share, and any post-handover share over 24 months. Escrow: at launch the developer posts the deposit in cash (default 30% of the construction budget, less work already done); the deposit either funds construction or stays locked to handover; any shortfall is met by equity; no distributions before handover; 5% of collections locked for 12 months after handover; future costs are reserved before each distribution. Debt: land loan at a share of the land price in month 0, construction facility drawn only when escrow cash runs short, up to its share of construction cost incurred, 1% arrangement fees, simple interest accrued and repaid with principal from free cash after handover. IRR: annualised from monthly equity flows. The engine was recomputed independently in Python and agrees to the dirham, levered and unlevered.
